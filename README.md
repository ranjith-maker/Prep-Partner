Your personal AI interview preparation partner.

Prep-Partner is an AI-integrated web application designed to help candidates prepare for interviews with confidence. Powered by Google's Gemini LLM and built using the MERN stack, it generates interview questions and detailed, interview-oriented answers for a wide range of roles.

Whether you're preparing for your first interview or exploring a new career opportunity, Prep-Partner helps you learn concepts, understand answers in depth, and revisit questions whenever you need them.

✨ Features
🤖 AI-Powered Interview Preparation — Uses Google's Gemini LLM to generate relevant interview questions and answers.
💼 Unlimited Role Exploration — Prepare for interviews across different job roles and domains.
📝 Customizable Question Generation — Generate as many interview questions as you need for your preparation.
🧠 In-Depth Explanations — Get detailed, easy-to-understand answers tailored to real interview scenarios rather than just textbook definitions.
🔄 Revisit Questions Anytime — Review previously generated questions and answers to reinforce your learning.
🎯 Flexible Learning Experience — Focus on the roles, concepts, and questions that matter most to your career goals.

⚙️ How It Works
Choose a role — Enter the job role or domain you want to prepare for.
Generate questions — Let Gemini AI generate relevant interview questions and answers.
Understand the answers — Learn through detailed explanations designed for interview preparation.
Review and practice — Revisit generated questions and answers whenever you want to strengthen your understanding.

Why Prep-Partner?
Traditional learning resources often provide definitions without explaining how to communicate a concept effectively in an interview.
Prep-Partner focuses on bridging that gap by providing detailed, practical explanations that help candidates understand concepts and prepare to answer interview questions with confidence.
